## Final prompt set

### Hero — opaque background

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: original photographic website hero background for an Indian food discovery brand. Wide cinematic landscape 16:9 composition, exceptionally realistic editorial food photography, overhead flat lay. A generous Indian dinner spread frames the edges of a matte dark charcoal stone table: an appetizing copper handi of chicken biryani with individual saffron rice grains and caramelised onions on the right; a rustic bowl of paneer butter masala and folded garlic naan on the left; small steel bowls of chutney, red onion, lime, a little coriander and subtle deep-red cotton napkin near the lower corners. Keep the middle third of the composition relatively quiet dark tabletop for centered white website typography to be added separately in code. Natural low side light, tactile textures, subtle steam above the biryani, sophisticated restaurant campaign quality. Warm, generous, delicious, authentic Indian food, realistic scale and utensils. No text, no logos, no watermark, no people, no illustration, no mockup, no UI. Deliver one high-quality landscape photograph.

Shot from three-quarter overhead, complete bowl in frame with generous transparent margin, authentic appetizing textures, soft natural studio light, restrained realistic contact shadow. Professional editorial food photography, no props, no text, no labels, no logos, no illustration. Transparent background.
